## What the result establishes

There are nonempty geometric residual and complete-condition populations; every
candidate yields a finite H100 residual vector. The smaller complete-feature
population maps explicitly to geometric rows and is split at every rejected-frame
gap. Geometry has a continuous admitted segment, but complete-feature sequences
are short and fragmented. No gap is repaired by joining surviving rows.

The target remains SENSOR EDP minus spatially aligned RLMB projected onto the
reference left unit normal on `0,5,...,100 m`, with positive meaning left.
RLMB is a pseudo-reference. Finite values and internally consistent source/log
checks do not prove physical accuracy, physical prediction-time availability,
reference independence or generalization. The raw geometry was not independently
recomputed in this review; the standalone validator establishes artifact/lineage
consistency, not recovery of an independently observed target.

The 5 m confidence bucket width is still an unconfirmed implementation assumption.
The reviewed interface-2.3 same-major compatibility rationale is not a deployed-
producer source trace. Native path length and confidence-vector length cannot
confirm the bucket constant. Carry the assumptions explicitly into later review
and attempt a focused producer trace before a real fit; do not guess new semantics.
