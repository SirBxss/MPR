## Fixed questions and denominators

The unit is an original retained candidate-audit row, grouped by its original
outing and recording. It is not every decoded EDP message, a new residual or
an eligible training frame. Retain original geometry/condition flags, complete
failure-code lists, all row/recording identities and original matched support.
"Complete" below means the original geometry-qualified condition mask: both
`residual_available` and `conditions_available`, with no replacement rule.

1. **Current-endpoint question:** Does the EDP source stamp equal its own current
   lower or upper odometry bracket endpoint? Require both endpoints and EDP
   source as integer decimal stamps, positive, ordered and bracketing EDP time.
   Missing/null/nonpositive/out-of-bracket/inverted brackets are not assessable,
   with explicit reason counts. Malformed non-decimal/types fail the diagnostic;
   never coerce a float or Boolean timestamp. No tolerance or offset is fitted.
2. Report complete-versus-endpoint 2-by-2 tables over all assessable rows, and
   separately over original geometry-ready rows without a declared
   `estimate_features_*` failure. The latter is a literal failure-code stratum,
   not proof that every other input was available. Record both mismatch types,
   failure codes and identities. Every excluded/not-assessable denominator stays
   visible; do not turn unknown into unequal. A mismatch is an observation, not
   automatic refutation of every speed mechanism: other failures can intervene.
3. Report `speed_uses_future_source_sample` presence and the stricter case in
   which it is the only failure and geometry is available. Keep other incomplete
   rows distinct. No old condition flag or reason is replaced.
4. **Clock structure:** count positive EDP/reference and odometry bracket stamps
   whose nanoseconds-within-second are divisible by the fixed 2^20 ns grid from
   the already examined case. Report all entries, missing/nonpositive entries,
   and distinct (recording identity, timestamp) pairs. No cross-recording sample
   matching, source-time search, seconds shifting, grid tuning or physical
   clock-identity/resolution inference. No direct velocity payloads are needed.

No hypothesis is required to hold to complete the audit. Success is complete
input validation, full original coverage and reconciled denominators/tables;
report counterexamples and unknowns even if every other outing disagrees.
There is no outcome-based stopping, best-window selection or success threshold
for feature adoption. Exact source-time equality does not uniquely prove physical
sample identity, measurement epoch or consumer availability.
